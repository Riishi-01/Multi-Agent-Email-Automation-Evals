# **ByteMart — Cancellation Policy** 

**Version:** 1.0 **Last Updated:** [DD/MM/YYYY] **Effective Date:** [DD/MM/YYYY] 

**Statutory map (for internal use):** Consumer Protection Act, 2019 (right to information, right to be heard) · Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 (Reg. 5 — disclosure of cancellation policy) · Indian Contract Act, 1872 (revocation of offer, breach of contract). 

## **1. Scope** 

1.1 This Cancellation Policy (“ **Cancellation Policy** ”) governs the conditions, process, and timelines applicable to the cancellation of Orders placed on the ByteMart Platform. 

1.2 This Cancellation Policy forms an integral part of the ByteMart Terms and Conditions. In the event of a conflict, this Cancellation Policy shall prevail for matters relating to Order cancellation. 

## **2. Stages of an Order** 

2.1 For the purpose of this Cancellation Policy, an Order passes through the following stages: 

- (a) **Order Placed** — the Buyer has placed the Order and payment (if pre-paid) has been authorised; 

- (b) **Order Confirmed** — ByteMart has accepted the Order and issued an Order Confirmation; 

- (c) **Order Packed** — the Product has been packed and is awaiting pickup by the logistics partner; 

- (d) **Order Dispatched** — the Product has been handed over to the logistics partner and is in transit; 

- (e) **Out for Delivery** — the Product is on the logistics partner’s vehicle for delivery to the Buyer’s address; 

- (f) **Delivered** — the Product has been delivered to and accepted at the Buyer’s address. 

## **3. Cancellation Window** 

3.1 A Buyer may request cancellation of an Order at any time **within the delivery window** — that is, from the moment the Order is placed until the Order is actually delivered to the Buyer (or refused at the door). Once the Order has been delivered, the cancellation pathway under this Policy shall no longer apply. 

3.2 The “ **delivery window** ” means the period commencing on Order placement and ending on the moment of actual delivery (or refusal of delivery) at the Buyer’s address. This includes all of the following stages: (a) Order Placed; (b) Order Confirmed; (c) Order Packed; (d) Order Dispatched (in transit); (e) Out for Delivery.

# **7. Cancellation of Pre-Orders & Back-Orders** 

7.1 Pre-Order and Back-Order Products may be cancelled at any time within the delivery window, in accordance with Clause 3. 

7.2 The refund for cancelled Pre-Order and Back-Order Products shall be processed in accordance with Clause 4. 

7.3 If the Pre-Order or Back-Order Product is delayed beyond the estimated dispatch date by more than fifteen (15) business days, the Buyer may, in addition to the right to cancel, request a refund with a reasonable compensation for the delay, where appropriate. 

# **8. Cancellation of Bulk / B2B Orders** 

8.1 Bulk Orders, corporate Orders, and B2B Orders may be subject to additional cancellation terms communicated at the time of Order placement, including but not limited to noncancellability of custom-configured Products. 

8.2 For the avoidance of doubt, all statutory rights of the Buyer under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, and other Applicable Law shall continue to apply. 

# **9. Effect of Cancellation on Promotional Benefits** 

9.1 If the cancelled Order was placed using a coupon, promotional code, cashback, or freebie, the benefit may be reversed or adjusted in accordance with the specific terms of the promotion. 

9.2 Cancelled Orders shall not be counted towards minimum-purchase thresholds for future promotions. 

# **10. Chargebacks & Disputes** 

10.1 If the Buyer initiates a chargeback or dispute with their bank or card issuer in respect of an Order that ByteMart has processed in good faith (including a delivered Order), ByteMart reserves the right to: (a) Dispute the chargeback with documentary evidence; (b) Suspend the Buyer’s account pending investigation; and (c) Pursue recovery of costs, including the Product price, shipping charges, and chargeback processing fees. 

10.2 Buyers are encouraged to contact ByteMart first to resolve any issue before initiating a chargeback. 

# **11. Cancellations & Consumer Rights** 

11.1 Nothing in this Cancellation Policy shall be construed to limit or override the statutory rights of the Buyer under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, including the right to: (a) Be protected against unfair trade practices; (b) Be informed of the cancellation policy prior to purchase; (c) Seek redressal against defective goods or deficient services. 

11.2 For the avoidance of doubt, the Buyer’s right to seek redressal before the consumer forums shall not be affected by this Cancellation Policy.
