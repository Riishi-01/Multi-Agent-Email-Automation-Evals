# **ByteMart — Legal & Policy Documentation Pack** 

## **About this Document** 

This pack contains the full set of legal and policy documents required to operate **ByteMart** — an Indian e-commerce platform retailing consumer electronics, gaming hardware, peripherals, software, and digital goods to end customers in India. 

The pack is drafted to be **citation-ready** (clauses numbered 1, 1.1, 1.1.1, …), **publicationready** (drop into your website, app, and checkout flow), and **audit-ready** (each clause maps to one or more statutory obligations).

## **1. Scope** 

1.1 This Payment and Pricing Policy (“ **Payment Policy** ”) governs the prices, taxes, payment methods, processing, security, and related matters for transactions on the ByteMart Platform. 

1.2 This Payment Policy forms an integral part of the ByteMart Terms and Conditions. In the event of a conflict, this Payment Policy shall prevail for matters relating to payments and pricing. 

## **2. Pricing** 

2.1 All prices displayed on the Platform are in **Indian Rupees (₹)** and are inclusive of all applicable taxes, including Goods and Services Tax (“ **GST** ”), unless expressly stated otherwise. 

2.2 Prices displayed on the Product page are the maximum retail price (“ **MRP** ”) as declared on the Product under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011. ByteMart may offer the Product at a price lower than the MRP. 

2.3 The price applicable to an Order is the price displayed at the time of Order placement. ByteMart reserves the right to modify prices at any time prior to Order acceptance. 

2.4 Despite ByteMart’s best efforts, a small number of Products may be mispriced. If a Product is listed at an incorrect price due to a technical or human error, ByteMart reserves the right to: (a) Cancel the Order and notify the Buyer of the cancellation, with a full refund; or (b) Contact the Buyer to confirm the corrected price before dispatch. 

2.5 ByteMart is not obligated to honour a mispriced Order. 

## **3. Taxes** 

3.1 GST is charged on all Products and on delivery charges, as applicable, in accordance with the **CGST Act, 2017** , and the relevant state GST laws.

3.2 The **HSN (Harmonised System of Nomenclature) code** for each Product is displayed on the Product page or in the tax invoice, as required. 

3.3 ByteMart shall issue a **tax invoice** in accordance with Rule 46 of the CGST Rules, 2017, containing: (a) Name, address, GSTIN, and PAN of ByteMart; (b) Invoice number and date; (c) Buyer’s name, billing address, shipping address, and GSTIN (if provided); (d) HSN code, description, quantity, unit price, and taxable value of each Product; (e) GST rate and amount (CGST, SGST/UTGST, or IGST, as applicable); (f) Total invoice value; (g) Place of supply; (h) Whether the transaction is intra-state, inter-state, or import. 

3.4 For B2B Buyers, GSTIN may be captured at checkout to enable issuance of a tax invoice and to support the Buyer’s input tax credit (“ **ITC** ”) claim, subject to verification. 

3.5 Where ByteMart acts as an **e-commerce operator** under Section 9(4) of the CGST Act, 2017, it shall collect tax at source (“ **TCS** ”) at the rate notified by the Government and deposit the same with the Government. The TCS amount shall be reflected in the tax invoice and in the Buyer’s GSTR-2A / 2B (as applicable). 

# **4. Payment Service Provider** 

4.1 ByteMart uses **Razorpay** (“ **Payment Service Provider** ” or “ **PSP** ”) as its payment gateway and payment service provider for all online (pre-paid) transactions on the Platform. 

4.2 The PSP processes payment transactions on behalf of ByteMart in compliance with the **Reserve Bank of India (“RBI”)** regulations, including the **Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007** , the **RBI Tokenisation Directions, 2021** , the **RBI Master Directions on Prepaid Payment Instruments (“PPIs”)** , 2021, and the **RBI Customer Protection Framework for Digital Payment Transactions, 2021** . 

4.3 ByteMart does not store your complete card or banking credentials. Such data is handled solely by the PSP in accordance with PCI-DSS and applicable RBI guidelines. 

4.4 By placing an Order, you agree to be bound by the PSP’s terms of service and privacy policy in addition to these Terms. The PSP’s terms are available on the Razorpay website. 

4.5 ByteMart is not a bank and does not itself hold or manage the Buyer’s funds. All payments are received and held by the PSP in its nodal/escrow account and remitted to ByteMart in accordance with RBI guidelines. 

# **5. Accepted Payment Methods** 

5.1 ByteMart accepts the following payment methods for pre-paid Orders, subject to availability and Buyer eligibility: 

- (a) **Debit Cards** — Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Maestro; 

- (b) **Credit Cards** — Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, American Express; 

- (c) **Unified Payments Interface (“UPI”)** . 

5.2 No other payment methods (including but not limited to net banking, mobile wallets, prepaid payment instruments, EMI, BNPL, or cheques) are accepted at this time. 

5.3 ByteMart reserves the right to add, modify, or remove payment methods at any time, with or without notice. 

5.4 Where a payment is made using a debit or credit card issued outside India, the transaction is subject to the RBI’s foreign exchange regulations and to the cross-border processing terms of the card network and the Buyer’s issuing bank.

# **6. Cash on Delivery (“COD”)** 

6.1 **Cash on Delivery is available** on the Platform subject to pincode serviceability, Order value limits, and verification by ByteMart or the PSP. 

6.2 An **additional non-refundable charge of ₹100 (Indian Rupees One Hundred only)** shall be levied on every COD Order (“ **COD Charge** ”). The COD Charge is a delivery-related service charge and is applicable to all COD Orders regardless of the value of the Order, the delivery location, or the mode of payment chosen for the Order value at the time of delivery. 

6.3 The COD Charge is clearly displayed in the Order summary at checkout, prior to Order placement. 

6.4 The COD Charge is **not refundable** in the event of: (a) Cancellation of the COD Order after dispatch (subject to the Cancellation Policy); (b) Refusal of the COD Order at the time of delivery; (c) Return-to-Origin (“RTO”) of the COD Order due to Buyer unavailability, refusal, or inability to pay. 

6.5 The COD Charge shall be **refunded** only where the COD Order is cancelled by ByteMart (e.g., out of stock, pricing error, non-serviceable pincode, or inability to verify the Order), in accordance with the Cancellation Policy. 

6.6 **Mode of payment for the Order value at the time of delivery:** The Buyer may pay the Order value (Product price + taxes) at the time of delivery using **UPI or Card** (debit or credit), as facilitated by the delivery agent on a handheld terminal or by any other means as communicated. The **₹100 COD Charge remains applicable in all such cases** , because the COD Charge is a delivery-related service charge and not a cash-handling charge. The COD Charge is independent of, and additional to, the mode of payment chosen by the Buyer for the Order value. 

6.7 COD Orders are subject to verification by ByteMart and/or the PSP. ByteMart reserves the right to: (a) Cancel any COD Order that fails verification, in accordance with the Cancellation Policy; (b) Impose a maximum Order value limit on COD Orders; (c) Restrict COD availability for specific Products, categories, or pincodes; (d) Withhold COD facility from Buyers with a history of COD refusals, RTOs, or chargebacks. 

6.8 In the event of non-payment, refusal, or inability to pay at the time of delivery, the Order shall be treated as RTO, and Clause 6.4 shall apply. ByteMart may, at its discretion, suspend the Buyer’s COD facility for future Orders. 

6.9 The PSP does not process the COD Order value; the COD Order value is collected by ByteMart’s logistics partner at the time of delivery. The PSP is engaged solely for verification, fraud-screening, and post-delivery reconciliation of COD Orders. 

# **7. Payment Security** 

7.1 All payment transactions on the Platform are processed through **PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment gateway providers** (Razorpay). ByteMart does not store your complete card or banking credentials on its servers. 

7.2 In compliance with the **RBI Tokenisation Directions, 2021** , ByteMart supports **card tokenisation** for repeat Customers. Tokens are stored by the payment gateway and/or the card network, not by ByteMart. 

7.3 All payment information is transmitted over **TLS 1.2 or higher** and protected by industrystandard encryption. 

7.4 ByteMart does not store CVV, full card numbers (except the last 4 digits, where required for display), or full bank account numbers.

# **8. Failed Transactions** 

8.1 If a payment is initiated but the transaction fails (e.g., due to insufficient funds, network error, or bank decline), the Order shall not be confirmed, and the Buyer’s account shall not be debited. 

8.2 In the event of a duplicate debit or an unauthorised debit, the Buyer should immediately contact [support@bytemart.in] with the transaction reference. ByteMart shall investigate and facilitate a refund in accordance with the Return, Refund and Replacement Policy and the RBI’s customer protection framework. 

8.3 ByteMart shall not be liable for delays or losses arising from failed transactions attributable to the Buyer’s bank, payment instrument issuer, or network provider. 

# **9. Pricing Disclosures (E-Commerce Rules, 2020)** 

9.1 In accordance with **Reg. 5 of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** , ByteMart displays the following information on every Product page and at checkout: 

- (a) Clear description of the Product; 

- (b) MRP and selling price; 

- (c) Total price payable (including taxes and delivery charges); 

- (d) Country of origin; 

- (e) Name and address of the seller (ByteMart);

9.2 No hidden charges are added at checkout. The total price displayed at the payment step is the final amount payable by the Buyer (subject to the COD Charge under Clause 6 where applicable). 

# **10. Discounts, Coupons & Promotional Offers** 

10.1 Discounts, coupons, cashback, and promotional offers are governed by the specific terms communicated at the time of the offer, including but not limited to: (a) Eligibility (Products, categories, Users); (b) Validity period; (c) Minimum order value (if any); (d) Maximum discount value (if any); (e) One-time or repeat use; (f) Combination with other offers; (g) Cancellation and refund effect (see the Cancellation Policy and the Return, Refund and Replacement Policy). 

10.2 ByteMart reserves the right to modify, withdraw, or cancel any offer at any time, with or without notice, in accordance with Applicable Law. 

10.3 Offers are not exchangeable for cash and are non-transferable unless expressly stated. 

10.4 Promotional discounts, where applicable, are applied only to the Product price and taxes, and not to the COD Charge or delivery charges, unless expressly stated. 

## **10.5 Promotional Sales — Limited Period, Limited Stock, No Price Difference Refund**

The start time, end time, and duration of each promotion are determined solely by ByteMart. 

10.5.2 **Limited Stock.** Promotional pricing is available **only for the limited stock** allocated to the promotion. ByteMart may, at its sole discretion, set per-User, per-Order, or per-Product purchase limits during a promotion.
