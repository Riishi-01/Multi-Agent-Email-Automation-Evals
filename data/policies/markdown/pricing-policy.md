## **10.4 Pre-Orders During Sales** 

10.4.1 During a promotional sale (including but not limited to festive sales, flash sales, anniversary sales, and clearance sales), ByteMart may, at its sole discretion, **accept or not accept** Pre-Orders for selected Products. 

10.4.2 Where Pre-Orders are accepted during a sale, the Buyer may place a Pre-Order through the Platform during the active promotional period. The Pre-Order will be honoured at the **promotional price** , subject to stock and the terms of the Pre-Order. 

10.4.3 Where Pre-Orders are **not accepted** during a sale, the Platform shall not display any “Pre-Order” or “Notify Me” option for the affected Product(s). The Buyer may purchase the Product only when (and if) it becomes available for regular sale at the regular price. 

10.4.4 For the avoidance of doubt, the general rules on offers and promotions in Clause 10 of the Payment and Pricing Policy apply to Pre-Orders placed during a sale.
