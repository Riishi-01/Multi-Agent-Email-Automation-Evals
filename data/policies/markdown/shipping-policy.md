1.1 This Shipping and Delivery Policy (“ **Shipping Policy** ”) governs the dispatch, transit, and delivery of all Products purchased on the ByteMart Platform. 

1.2 This Shipping Policy forms an integral part of the ByteMart Terms and Conditions. In the event of a conflict, this Shipping Policy shall prevail for matters relating to shipping and delivery. 

## **2. Delivery Options** 

2.1 ByteMart offers the following delivery options, subject to serviceability of the delivery pincode: 

- (a) **Fast Delivery** — a paid, expedited delivery option; 

- (b) **Regular Delivery** — a complimentary, standard delivery option. 

2.2 The availability of each option may vary by Product, Seller, and delivery pincode. Not all options may be available for all Orders. 

2.3 Delivery days are counted in **business days** , excluding Sundays and public holidays declared in the Buyer’s state of delivery.

# **3. Fast Delivery** 

3.1 **Charge:** Between **₹100 and ₹250** , depending on the Buyer’s delivery location. The exact Fast Delivery charge applicable to an Order is displayed at checkout before payment. 

3.2 **Delivery Timeframe: 3–4 business days** from the date of Order Confirmation. 

3.3 **Guarantee & Refund of Fast Delivery Charge:** If a Fast Delivery Order is not delivered within the guaranteed 3–4 business day window, the Buyer may request a **refund of the Fast Delivery charge only** (not the Product price, taxes, or other charges) by emailing [support@bytemart.in] with the Order number. Such refund requests shall be processed in accordance with the Return, Refund and Replacement Policy, within 3–7 business days of verification. 

3.4 **Exclusions from the Fast Delivery Guarantee:** The guarantee does not apply in cases of: (a) Force majeure events (see Clause 11 of the Terms); (b) Inaccurate or incomplete delivery address provided by the Buyer; (c) Buyer unavailability at the delivery address after reasonable delivery attempts; (d) Refusal to accept delivery; (e) Government-imposed restrictions, lockdowns, or curfews in the delivery area; (f) Non-serviceable or restricted pincode, where Fast Delivery was inadvertently displayed. 

3.5 **Order Cut-off:** Fast Delivery Orders placed after the daily cut-off time (as displayed on the Product/checkout page) shall be deemed placed on the next business day for the purpose of computing the delivery window.

# **7. Delivery Attempts** 

7.1 The logistics partner shall make up to **three (3) delivery attempts** at the address specified in the Order. 

7.2 If the Buyer is unavailable at the time of the delivery attempts, the logistics partner may contact the Buyer on the registered phone number to schedule a re-attempt. 

7.3 If delivery cannot be completed after three (3) attempts or within seven (7) days of the first attempt (whichever is earlier), the Order shall be **returned to origin (“RTO”)** . 

7.4 In the event of an RTO: (a) The Order shall be treated as cancelled; (b) Refund shall be processed in accordance with the Return, Refund and Replacement Policy, after deduction of any return shipping charges, if applicable; and (c) The Fast Delivery charge shall not be refunded if the non-delivery is attributable to the Buyer’s unavailability or refusal. 

# **8. Risk of Loss & Title** 

8.1 The risk of loss and title to each Product shall pass to the Buyer upon successful delivery to the address specified in the Order and the Buyer’s (or any adult at the address’s) acknowledgement of receipt. 

8.2 Until such acknowledgement, ByteMart bears the risk of loss in transit, subject to Clause 9 (Damaged, Incomplete, or Wrong Orders). 

# **9. Damaged, Incomplete, or Wrong Orders** 

9.1 The Buyer is requested to **inspect the Product at the time of delivery** and report any issue (physical damage, missing item, or wrong item) at the doorstep or within **forty-eight (48) hours** of delivery, whichever is earlier. 

9.2 Reporting channels: (a) Email: [support@bytemart.in] (b) “My Orders” →“Report an Issue” 

9.3 Issues must be reported within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of delivery in accordance with the Return, Refund and Replacement Policy. Issues reported beyond the 14-day Reporting Window may not be eligible for return, replacement, or refund, except in cases covered by applicable manufacturer warranty. 

9.4 Resolution of damaged, incomplete, or wrong Orders is governed by the **Return, Refund and Replacement Policy** . 

# **10. Pre-Orders & Back-Orders** 

10.1 Products marked as “ **Pre-Order** ” or “ **Back-Order** ” are not immediately available for delivery. The estimated dispatch date is displayed on the Product page. 

10.2 The delivery timeframe for Pre-Order and Back-Order Products shall be communicated separately and may be longer than the standard Fast or Regular Delivery windows. 

10.3 Pre-Order and Back-Order Products are subject to the **Cancellation Policy** and may be cancelled at any time within the delivery window.
