# **3. Reporting Window** 

3.1 Any replacement or refund request under Clause 2 must be submitted to ByteMart **by email** within **fourteen (14) calendar days** of delivery (“ **Reporting Window** ”). 

3.2 Reporting channel (for replacement and refund requests only): (a) **Email:** [support@bytemart.in] (mandatory channel for replacement and refund requests) 

For general queries (order tracking, delivery status, account questions), Buyers may continue to use: (b) **Portal:** “My Orders” →“Report an Issue” on the Platform (for status / tracking) (c) **Phone:** [PHONE] (for general queries, not for replacement/refund requests) 

3.3 The replacement or refund request email must include, **as a mandatory requirement** : (a) Order number; (b) Description of the issue; (c) **Photographs of the box** (outer packaging) showing the Product inside, the tamper seal/condition, and the shipping label; (d) **Photographs of the Product** clearly showing the damage, defect, missing accessories, wrong version, or wrong item (close-up shots and wide shots); (e) Photograph of the **serial number / IMEI / model number** label of the Product (where applicable); (f) Photograph of all contents of the box (in case of missing items); and (g) Short video (optional but recommended for high-value items). 

3.4 **Photographs and attachments are mandatory** for any refund or replacement request for a delivered item. A return, refund, or replacement request submitted **without photographs of both the box and the Product** shall be deemed incomplete and shall not be processed until such photographs are provided. ByteMart may, at its sole discretion, request additional photographs or video evidence to substantiate the claim. 

3.5 Replacement or refund requests submitted **after the 14-day Reporting Window** may not be eligible for return, replacement, or refund, except in cases covered by an applicable manufacturer warranty. 

3.6 The Buyer must retain the Product, all original packaging, all accessories, all documentation, and the shipping label until the return, refund, or replacement is fully processed. Failure to retain the Product or its packaging may result in rejection of the claim. 

# **4. Resolution: Replacement** 

4.1 For Products reported under Clause 2, ByteMart’s default resolution is **replacement** of the Product, subject to stock availability of the same SKU (or an equivalent Product of equal or greater value, at ByteMart’s discretion). 

4.2 Replacement is offered **free of cost** , including: (a) No additional Product price; (b) No shipping or reverse-shipping charges. 

4.3 If the Buyer refuses the replacement, ByteMart is not obligated to offer a refund in lieu of replacement, except where the replacement is unavailable (see Clause 5).

7.1 Refunds for Orders cancelled in accordance with the Cancellation Policy shall be processed **without any charge** to the Buyer. 

7.2 The refund amount shall include: (a) The Product price; (b) Taxes charged on the Product; (c) Original delivery charges, if any (subject to the Cancellation Policy).

# **8. No Refund for Product Damage** 

8.1 ByteMart **does not offer monetary refunds for damaged Products** . Damaged Products are eligible for **replacement only** , in accordance with Clause 4. 

8.2 A monetary refund shall be offered only if the Product or its replacement is **out of stock** (Clause 5). 

8.3 This clause is without prejudice to any rights or remedies available to the Buyer under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or any other Applicable Law. 

# **9. Return Pickup & Reverse Logistics** 

9.1 For approved returns, ByteMart shall arrange reverse pickup from the Buyer’s delivery address at no cost to the Buyer, typically within 3–5 business days of approval. 

9.2 The Buyer shall: (a) Keep the Product in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, warranty cards, and freebies intact; (b) Affix the return label provided by ByteMart (where applicable); (c) Hand over the Product to the logistics partner during the scheduled pickup. 

9.3 If reverse pickup is not serviceable at the Buyer’s pincode, the Buyer may be required to self-ship the Product. ByteMart shall reimburse reasonable self-shipping costs upon verification, in accordance with our internal policy. 

# **10. Condition of Returned Products** 

10.1 The returned Product must be in the same condition as delivered, with all original packaging, accessories, manuals, warranty cards, and freebies. 

10.2 Products that show signs of use, damage caused after delivery, missing accessories, or tampering shall be rejected and returned to the Buyer without refund or replacement. 

10.3 ByteMart reserves the right to inspect the returned Product before processing a refund or replacement.
