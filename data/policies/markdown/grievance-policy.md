# **1. Scope** 

1.1 This Grievance Redressal Policy (“ **Grievance Policy** ”) describes the mechanism by which ByteMart receives, processes, and resolves complaints, grievances, and queries from Users in connection with the Platform, Products, Orders, deliveries, payments, refunds, and privacy. 

1.2 This Grievance Policy is published in compliance with: (a) **Regulation 4 of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** (appointment of grievance officer and grievance redressal mechanism); (b) **Regulation 4(2) of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021** (grievance officer for intermediaries); (c) **Section 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (right to grievance redressal by Data Principal); (d) **Section 35 of the Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (consumer dispute redressal forums). 

1.3 This Grievance Policy forms an integral part of the ByteMart Terms and Conditions.

# **4. Acknowledgement & Response Timelines** 

4.1 ByteMart shall **acknowledge** receipt of every grievance within **forty-eight (48) hours** of receipt, by email or SMS to the contact details provided by the User. 

- 4.2 ByteMart shall endeavour to **resolve** every grievance within **thirty (30) days** of receipt, in accordance with the DPDPA and the E-Commerce Rules, 2020. 

4.3 For specific categories of grievances, the following internal timelines shall apply:

# **6. Statutory Consumer Redressal Forums** 

6.1 Nothing in this Grievance Policy limits the User’s right to seek redressal from the statutory forums established under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019. ByteMart shall cooperate with such forums in good faith. 

## **6.2 National Consumer Helpline (NCH)** 

- (a) The National Consumer Helpline is a toll-free helpline operated by the Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India. 

- (b) **Helpline number: 1915** (toll-free, available in multiple languages). 

- (c) Users may file a complaint by dialling 1915 or via the NCH website at https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/ 

## **6.3 INGRAM Portal** 

- (a) The **INGRAM (Integrated Grievance Redressal Mechanism) portal** is the centralised consumer complaint platform of the Government of India. 

- (b) **URL:** https://consumerhelpline.gov.in/consumer/index.php/ingram-portal 

- (c) Users may lodge complaints online, track status, and receive redressal through this portal. 

## **6.4 e-Daakhil Portal** 

- (a) The **e-Daakhil portal** is the official portal for filing consumer complaints electronically before the Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions. 

- (b) **URL:** https://edaakhil.nic.in/
