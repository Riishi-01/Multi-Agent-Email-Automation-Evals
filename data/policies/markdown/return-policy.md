## **1. Scope** 

1.1 This Return, Refund and Replacement Policy (“ **Return Policy** ”) governs the conditions, process, and timelines applicable to returns, refunds, and replacements of Products purchased on the ByteMart Platform. 

1.2 This Return Policy forms an integral part of the ByteMart Terms and Conditions. In the event of a conflict, this Return Policy shall prevail for matters relating to returns, refunds, and replacements. 

## **2. Eligible Reasons for Return** 

2.1 ByteMart accepts return requests **only** in the following circumstances: 

- (a) **Physical Damage** — the Product is delivered in a damaged condition (cracked, broken, dented, water-damaged, or otherwise physically compromised); 

- (b) **Incomplete Order** — one or more items in a multi-item Order are missing at the time of delivery; or 

- (c) **Wrong Product** — the Product delivered does not match the Product ordered (different model, variant, colour, size, or configuration). 

2.2 Returns for reasons other than those listed in Clause 2.1 (e.g., “change of mind”, buyer’s remorse, or non-defect-related dissatisfaction) are **not** accepted, except where required by Applicable Law.
